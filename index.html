Welcome to the Anatomy Department

The Department of Human Anatomy at Bingham University is committed to providing a strong foundation in the study of the structure and organization of the human body.

About the Department

Human Anatomy is at the heart of medical education. It provides the structural knowledge necessary for understanding how the human body is organized and how its various systems relate to one another.

Our department provides students with opportunities to explore anatomy through lectures, practical sessions, laboratory experiences, research, and other forms of academic engagement. From the microscopic structure of tissues to the complex organization of the human body, our goal is to make the study of anatomy both meaningful and applicable.

Our Mission

To provide quality anatomical education while encouraging academic excellence, scientific curiosity, research, and professional development.
